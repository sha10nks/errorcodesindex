<p class="c1"><span class="c0">Title</span></p>
<p class="c1"><span class="c0">(AK9-X — Rejected, Content After Decryption Could Not Be Analyzed)</span></p>
<p class="c1"><span class="c0">One-sentence summary</span></p>
<p class="c1"><span class="c0">AK9-X indicates the receiver could not parse the decrypted content for the group, so the functional group was rejected.</span></p>

<p class="c1"><span class="c0">What this code means</span></p>
<p class="c1"><span class="c0">AK9 “X” is a group-level rejection tied to encryption/decryption handling or post-decryption parsing. It usually indicates mismatched encryption settings, a corrupted payload, or an incompatible security configuration.</span></p>

<p class="c1"><span class="c0">Step-by-step fix</span></p>
<p class="c1"><span class="c0">1) Confirm you encrypted to the partner’s current public key/certificate.</span></p>
<p class="c1"><span class="c0">2) Verify signing/encryption algorithms and any compression settings required by the partner.</span></p>
<p class="c1"><span class="c0">3) Re-send a small test interchange/group and compare security logs on both ends.</span></p>
<p class="c1"><span class="c0">4) If you use an intermediary clearinghouse, confirm which hop is rejecting the payload.</span></p>
