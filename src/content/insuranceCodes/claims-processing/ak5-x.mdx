<p class="c1"><span class="c0">Title</span></p>
<p class="c1"><span class="c0">(AK5-X — Rejected, Content After Decryption Could Not Be Analyzed)</span></p>
<p class="c1"><span class="c0">One-sentence summary</span></p>
<p class="c1"><span class="c0">AK5-X indicates the receiver could not parse the decrypted content, so the transaction set was rejected.</span></p>

<p class="c1"><span class="c0">What this code means</span></p>
<p class="c1"><span class="c0">AK5 “X” is a rejection tied to encryption/decryption handling. It typically indicates a mismatch in encryption settings, corrupted payload, or a partner-side parsing failure after decryption.</span></p>

<p class="c1"><span class="c0">Step-by-step fix</span></p>
<p class="c1"><span class="c0">1) Confirm encryption settings match the partner’s requirements (algorithm, key/cert, signing vs encryption).</span></p>
<p class="c1"><span class="c0">2) Verify certificate chains and that the correct public key was used for encryption.</span></p>
<p class="c1"><span class="c0">3) Re-send a small test payload and compare logs on both sides if possible.</span></p>
<p class="c1"><span class="c0">4) If the transport is AS2 or similar, confirm MIC/signature verification results.</span></p>

<p class="c1"><span class="c0">Related error codes</span></p>
