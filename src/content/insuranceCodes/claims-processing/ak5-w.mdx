<p class="c1"><span class="c0">CODE: AK5-W</span></p>
<p class="c1"><span class="c0">Title</span></p>
<p class="c1"><span class="c0">(AK5-W — Rejected, Assurance Failed Validity Tests)</span></p>
<p class="c1"><span class="c0">One-sentence summary</span></p>
<p class="c1"><span class="c0">AK5-W indicates the transaction set was rejected due to security/assurance validation failure.</span></p>

<p class="c1"><span class="c0">What this code means</span></p>
<p class="c1"><span class="c0">AK5 “W” is a rejection tied to assurance/security validity checks. In real workflows, treat it as a transport/security problem until proven otherwise.</span></p>

<p class="c1"><span class="c0">Step-by-step fix</span></p>
<p class="c1"><span class="c0">1) Confirm you are sending through the correct secure channel and partner endpoint.</span></p>
<p class="c1"><span class="c0">2) Validate certificates/keys and any signing/encryption settings required by the partner.</span></p>
<p class="c1"><span class="c0">3) Review translator/security logs for exact validation failure details.</span></p>
<p class="c1"><span class="c0">4) Coordinate with the trading partner to confirm expected security configuration.</span></p>
