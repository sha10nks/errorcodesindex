+<p class="c1"><span class="c0">What this code means</span></p>
+<p class="c1"><span class="c0">Letter 5747C is an identity verification letter that may require an in-person appointment or specific verification steps as described in the notice. It is used when the IRS needs higher-confidence verification before continuing. Follow the letter’s instructions closely and use only official IRS channels.</span></p>
