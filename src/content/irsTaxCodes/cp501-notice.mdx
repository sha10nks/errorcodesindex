+<p class="c1"><span class="c0">One-sentence summary</span></p>
+<p class="c1"><span class="c0">CP501 is a balance due reminder notice indicating the IRS believes you have an unpaid amount for a tax year.</span></p>
+<p class="c1"><span class="c0">What this code means</span></p>
+<p class="c1"><span class="c0">CP501 is a reminder notice for an existing balance due on an account. It usually follows earlier balance due communication and is intended to prompt payment or contact. The key actions are to confirm the tax year, verify whether the balance is accurate, and resolve it through payment, a plan, or a documented dispute.</span></p>

<p class="c1"><span class="c0">Identity verification or fraud screening triggers</span></p>
<p class="c1"><span class="c0">Balance due, penalty, or adjustment notifications</span></p>
+<p class="c1"><span class="c0">Step-by-step troubleshooting/fix</span></p>
+<p class="c1"><span class="c0">Read the notice header, tax year, and response deadline first.</span></p>
<p class="c1"><span class="c0">Confirm the tax year and amount in your IRS account or transcript.</span></p>
<p class="c1"><span class="c0">If paid already, gather proof and contact the IRS through official channels.</span></p>
<p class="c1"><span class="c0">If you owe, pay or set up an agreement before penalties/interest increase.</span></p>
<p class="c1"><span class="c0">Keep copies of everything you submit and proof of delivery/submission.</span></p>
