+<p class="c1"><span class="c0">What this code means</span></p>
+<p class="c1"><span class="c0">Letter 12C is commonly used to request missing information or documents needed to process a return. This often includes unsigned forms, missing schedules, or documentation supporting credits/withholding. Your response quality matters: submit exactly what is requested using the address and instructions on the letter and keep proof.</span></p>

<p class="c1"><span class="c0">Identity verification or fraud screening triggers</span></p>
<p class="c1"><span class="c0">Balance due, penalty, or adjustment notifications</span></p>
+<p class="c1"><span class="c0">Step-by-step troubleshooting/fix</span></p>
+<p class="c1"><span class="c0">Read the notice header, tax year, and response deadline first.</span></p>
<p class="c1"><span class="c0">Identify exactly what the letter is requesting and respond with those items only.</span></p>
<p class="c1"><span class="c0">Make copies of everything you send and use trackable delivery if mailing.</span></p>
<p class="c1"><span class="c0">If the requested item is not applicable, respond with a clear written explanation and supporting proof.</span></p>
<p class="c1"><span class="c0">Keep copies of everything you submit and proof of delivery/submission.</span></p>
