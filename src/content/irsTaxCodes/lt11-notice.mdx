+<p class="c1"><span class="c0">What this code means</span></p>
+<p class="c1"><span class="c0">LT11 is a final notice of intent to levy. Like other final levy notices, it includes a response deadline and information about your rights. Treat it as urgent: confirm the balance and take action promptly, especially if you plan to dispute or request a hearing.</span></p>
