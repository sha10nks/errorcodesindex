+<p class="c1"><span class="c0">What this code means</span></p>
+<p class="c1"><span class="c0">Letter 4883C is an IRS identity verification request. It indicates the IRS needs additional confirmation before proceeding with processing. The letter’s instructions and verification channel matter; follow the letter exactly and use only official IRS contact methods. Verification requests commonly delay refunds until completed.</span></p>

+<p class="c1"><span class="c0">Step-by-step troubleshooting/fix</span></p>
+<p class="c1"><span class="c0">Read the notice header, tax year, and response deadline first.</span></p>
<p class="c1"><span class="c0">Use the official contact method listed on the letter and avoid third-party numbers.</span></p>
<p class="c1"><span class="c0">Collect: return copy, W-2/1099 info, and prior-year filing facts.</span></p>
<p class="c1"><span class="c0">Do not file a second return “just in case”; duplicates can increase delays.</span></p>
<p class="c1"><span class="c0">Keep copies of everything you submit and proof of delivery/submission.</span></p>
