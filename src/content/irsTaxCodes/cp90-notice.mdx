+<p class="c1"><span class="c0">What this code means</span></p>
+<p class="c1"><span class="c0">CP90 is a final notice of intent to levy and provides information about your right to request a collection due process (CDP) hearing. This is a serious collection-stage notice with strict deadlines. The safest path is to verify the balance, review options (pay, plan, dispute, or hearing request), and respond within the timeframe stated on the notice.</span></p>
